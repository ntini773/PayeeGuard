POST/api/loginSign in with a marketplace account
POST/api/logoutEnd the session
GET/api/v1/destination-change-requestsThe signed-in seller's own requests, newest first
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requestsAsk to change the payout account
GET/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}One request. Reviewers get extra fields.
GET/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/summaryNext steps. Reviewers also get claims, evidence and policy findings.
GET/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/eventsAppend-only audit trail
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/messagesAdd a note. Treated as an untrusted claim.
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/auth/{purpose}Start a PayPal login to verify the NEW or the OLD account
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/recoveryAsk for a recovery review when the old account cannot be verified
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/cancelCancel an open or waiting request
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/reviewApprove or reject. Reviewers only.
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/evidenceRecord what the marketplace's records show (prototype stand-in, browser reviewers only)
POST/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/ai-reviewStart a read-only AI review
GET/api/v1/destination-change-requests/{caseId}/ai-review/{runId}Steps of a review so far
GET/api/v1/casesTenant queue for reviewers, newest first
GET/api/v1/paypal/callbackPayPal returns here after a login. Server-side exchange only.
POST/api/v1/seller-guideHelp chat for sellers. No tools, no case data, no authority.
GET/api/v1/marketplace-payoutsStored earnings and their payout receipts
